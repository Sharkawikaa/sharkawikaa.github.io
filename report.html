Performance Narrative

✅ Successes & What Worked

We saw a strong month overall, finishing well above our baseline ROAS targets. The newly introduced Advantage+ Shopping Campaign (ASC+) structure allowed us to stabilize the CPA.

Key Driver: The "Unboxing" video creative format performed exceptionally well, achieving a high hook-rate which lowered our overall Cost Per Click (CPC) to healthy levels.

⚠️ Friction & Key Learnings

While the overall CPA is healthy at 176 EGP, our Top-of-Funnel static image ads suffered from creative fatigue towards the end of the month, causing CPA to spike slightly in week 4.

Action Taken: We paused the fatigued static ads and are re-allocating that budget toward fresh video creatives and dynamic catalog ads.

  • Scale budget on ASC+ campaign by 15% to capitalize on winning video creatives.
